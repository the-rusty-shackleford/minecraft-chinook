## The dimensions

The numbers the model is built to (metres):

- published: two rotors of 18.29 across, three blades each; the fuselage 3.78 across its fuel pods;
  the cabin inside 9.30 long, 2.29 wide and 1.98 high, with a full-width ramp at the back;
- a real CH-47 airframe as measured for shipping (Scroggins Aviation's CH-47 data sheet, 2023):
  about 15.98 long, 3.81 to the outsides of the front wheels, 5.79 from the ground to the top of the
  aft pylon, the belly about 0.64 up;
- read off the 3-view's side view at the scale its 15.9 fuselage gives (0.0628 a pixel), which puts
  the rotor hubs 11.95 apart (published: 11.94) and the aft hub 5.6 up: the forward hub 1.95 behind
  the nose and 4.58 up; the forward pylon's top 4.02; the aft pylon's 4.96; the roof 3.3; the
  portholes 2.09 up; the wheels 6.1 and 12.5 behind the nose. The 3-view's front and top views are not
  drawn to its side view's scale, so the widths are the published ones above.
